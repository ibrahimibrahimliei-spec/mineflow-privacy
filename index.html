Privacy Policy

You do not create an account and we never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress is stored on your device. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted too, unless you signed in to Google Play Games, which keeps a copy of it in your Google account (Saved Games).

3. Ads and your choices

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads; you can change your choice at any time from the game's settings. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads). A one-time "Remove Ads" purchase turns off interstitial ads; optional rewarded ads you choose to watch stay available.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

5. Third-party services

These services have their own privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy · AdMob · Firebase · Sentry · Google Play Games Services · Google Payments.

6. Data retention and deletion

Analytics data is kept by Firebase for up to 14 months; crash reports for up to 90 days. Leaderboard, country race and invite entries are kept for 12 months. You can ask us to delete any data linked to your device or your anonymous player ID (shown in the game's settings as your invite code) by emailing ibrahimibrahimli.ei@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Your cloud save can be deleted in the Google Play Games app. Uninstalling the game removes all progress stored on your device.

7. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete related data.

8. Security

Data sent to our service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
