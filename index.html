In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads; you can change your choice at any time from the game's settings. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads). A one-time "Remove Ads" purchase turns off interstitial ads; optional rewarded ads you choose to watch stay available.

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

These services have their own privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy · AdMob · Firebase · Google Payments.

Analytics data is kept by Firebase for up to 14 months; crash reports for up to 90 days. You can ask us to delete any data linked to your device by emailing [CONTACT EMAIL] with the subject "Delete my data". Uninstalling the game removes all progress stored on your device.

7. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete related data.

8. Security

Data sent to our service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
